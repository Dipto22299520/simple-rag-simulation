The Greenfield College library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:30 PM on weekdays. On Saturdays it closes early at 6:00 PM, and it is closed all day on Sundays.

Students can borrow up to 7 books at a time. The standard loan period is 21 days, and each book can be renewed twice online through the GreenPortal app.

Overdue books cost 25 cents per day in late fees, up to a maximum of $5 per book. Students with more than $10 in unpaid fees cannot borrow new books.

Group study rooms are on the third floor and can be booked for a maximum of 2 hours per day. Food is not allowed in the study rooms, but drinks in sealed bottles are fine.
