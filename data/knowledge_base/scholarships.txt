The Aurora Merit Scholarship gives $2,000 per year to students with a GPA of 3.6 or higher. The application deadline is March 15 every year.

The Helping Hands Grant supports students from low-income families. It covers 50 percent of tuition fees and requires a family income certificate and one recommendation letter.

Students who receive any scholarship must complete 20 hours of community service each semester. Failing to complete the service hours means the scholarship is paused for the next semester.

International students are eligible for the Global Bridge Award, worth $1,500, if they have completed at least one full year at Greenfield College.
