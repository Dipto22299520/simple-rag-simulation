Students must arrive at the exam hall at least 15 minutes before the exam starts. Anyone who arrives more than 30 minutes late is not allowed to enter.

Only the Casio FX-82 calculator is permitted in exams. Phones, smartwatches and phone calculator apps are strictly forbidden and must be left in the bags at the front of the hall.

A student who fails a course may retake the exam once, during the summer retake week in July. The maximum grade for a retake exam is capped at a C.

Exam results are published on GreenPortal exactly 10 working days after the exam. Students who want a re-check of their paper must apply within 5 days of the results being published.
