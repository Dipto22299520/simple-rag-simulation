The main cafeteria, called The Green Bowl, serves breakfast from 7:30 AM to 10:00 AM and lunch from 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM. Every Friday is Pizza Friday, when a slice costs only $1.

The student Wi-Fi network is called GF-Student. Its password changes every Monday morning and is written on the whiteboard at the IT help desk in Building C.

A student parking permit costs $45 per semester and can be bought at the security office. Cars without a permit parked on campus are fined $30.

There are more than 40 student clubs. The most popular are the Robotics Club, which meets on Wednesdays, and the Astronomy Club, which holds night-sky sessions on the roof every second Thursday.
